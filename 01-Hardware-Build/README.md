
## Physical Server Assembly

### Step 1 – Case Preparation

Here i unboxed the Lian Li LANCOOL 217 and prepared the case for hardware installation, making sure to not lose any screws or anything important for the build.

<img src="./%20images/01%20Case%20Unboxing.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 2 – CPU Installation

Installed the AMD Ryzen 7 7700 into the AM5 motherboard socket, ensuring the processor was correctly aligned with the triangle marking before securing it in place.

<img src="./%20images/02%20CPU%20Installation.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 3 – RAM Installation

Installed the Kingston Fury Beast DDR5 memory kit, consisting of two 16GB modules, Here i used the A2 and B2 slots for dual-channel operation and better stability for the RAM. 

<img src="./%20images/03%20Ram%20Installation.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 4 – Motherboard and Storage Preparation

Prepared the ASUS TUF Gaming B650E-E WiFi motherboard and installed the Lexar NM990 2TB PCIe Gen5 NVMe SSD which was pretty simple, only needed to remove two screws to insert it in the slot. 

<img src="./%20images/04%20Motherboard%20Preperation.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 5 – CPU Cooler Installation

Installed the AMD Wraith Prism CPU cooler and connected the fan to the motherboard's CPU_FAN header which provides cooling for the CPU.

<img src="./%20images/05%20CPU%20Fan%20installation.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 6 – Cable Management

Here i organised and routed the power supply and case cables to improve airflow, reduce clutter and make it easier for me for future hardware maintenance and upgrades.

<img src="./%20images/06%20Cable%20Management.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 7 – Internal Assembly

Completed the internal hardware assembly, including the motherboard, power supply, memory, storage and fan components.

<img src="./%20images/07%20Internal%20Assembly.jpg" width="650">

---

### Step 8 – Completed Server

Successfully assembled and powered on the dedicated home server, ready for BIOS configuration and Proxmox VE installation.
